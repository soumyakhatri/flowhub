Phase gate

Do not skip ahead. Each phase has to answer these before implementation:

  1. What problem appeared?
  2. Why is this the right tool?
  3. What are the trade-offs?
  4. How do we fail and recover?
  5. How do we observe it?

Then update this page, ROADMAP.md, and the learning log.
